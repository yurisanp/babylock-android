Your child watches. You relax.

You hand your phone to your child for a cartoon and, seconds later, the video is paused, another app is open or someone is getting a call. BabyLock fixes that: it locks touches on the screen while the video keeps playing.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Open BabyLock and tap "Lock".
2. Open the video within 5 seconds.
3. Hand over the phone. Little fingers can't change a thing.

To unlock, hold the top right corner for a few seconds and confirm with your fingerprint, face, phone password or a PIN. A child tapping the screen won't unlock it.

ABOUT THE ACCESSIBILITY PERMISSION
BabyLock uses the Android accessibility service only to lock touches, gestures and volume while the lock is on, and to close the notification shade. It only checks whether the shade is open, never reads text or other apps, and nothing it sees leaves your phone. If you prefer not to turn on accessibility, a simpler mode locks touches only.

Note: no app can lock the power button. Restarting the phone always unlocks it.
